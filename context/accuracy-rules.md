# Accuracy Rules (MANDATORY)

Every skill that touches resume, cover-letter, or outreach content follows this file exactly.
It is the standing constraint that keeps every generated document something you could defend,
unprepared, in an interview. Fill the "Your rules" sections in once; the agent will never
argue with them.

## Overarching rule: no lies, no over-exaggeration

Every bullet and competency claim must be something you could speak to confidently without
preparation. When in doubt, scope down: "contributed to" is safer than "led"; "supported" is
safer than "owned". The agent never invents a qualification to clear a gate; it recommends
skip or track-only instead.

## Scope of ownership (your rules)

Write down what you actually owned versus supported in each role, in the phrasing you want
used and the phrasing you never want used. Example shape:

- Use: "dedicated PM for adoption strategy for <product>"
- Never: "sole PM for <product>", "defined the product roadmap for <product>"

## Hyperbole blacklist (your rules)

Phrases that must never appear without direct supporting evidence, e.g. "raised the
enterprise bar", "at global scale". Replace with "informed", "supported", "contributed to".

## Domain claims (your rules)

Domains where your knowledge is adjacent rather than practitioner-level, and the exact
phrasing to use instead (e.g. "security-adjacent networking experience", not "cybersecurity
domain expertise").

## Job title accuracy

Titles are exactly as your employer recorded them. Describe the nature of the work in bullets,
never by retitling. Record any company name changes so two names for one employer are never
presented as two jobs.

## Resume date formatting

Single-role companies: dates on the company line only. Multi-role companies: each title line
gets its own sub-role date range.

## Voice

No em dashes in anything a person other than you will read (resumes, cover letters, outreach,
referral asks). Interpersonal messages read like you wrote them: plain conversational prose,
no bold headers, no bullet "findings".

## Referral / warm-outreach voice

Messages to people you already know are written like a text to a friend: name the role and
company, one line on why it fits, a low-pressure ask. Same truthfulness bar as a resume bullet.

## Resume baseline (MANDATORY)

Always start tailored resumes from the master file (path in `config.md`). Never from a
previously tailored version; tailored versions accumulate narrative drift.

## Employer AI-use policies

Generic careers-site boilerplate about AI use is your call (record your standing decision
here). An employer-specific candidate AI policy with a required disclosure field is different:
read it before filling any field; free-text answers start as your own draft; a cover letter
written from scratch by the agent falls on the wrong side of such a policy, so omit it.
